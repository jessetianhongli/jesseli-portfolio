Meetup Creation Flow

Role  UX DesignerCompany  Dyne TechnologiesTimeline  2020–2022
−23%user drop-off

The problem

Dyne's app is built around in-person meetups. Creating a new meetup asked people for too much up front, location, capacity, tags, a cover photo, before they'd gotten any payoff, and a lot of people started the flow and never finished it.

What I worked on

I rebuilt the meetup creation flow from the ground up, focused on getting someone to a published meetup as fast as possible and treating everything else as optional, addable later.

  • Mapped every field in the old flow against how often it actually got filled in, and cut or deferred the ones that mostly sat empty
  • Reordered the flow so the meetup could be published after the essentials, with details editable afterward
  • Tested the new flow with a small group of existing organizers before rolling it out
  • Worked with the founding team on how this fed into the broader product story used to help close the seed round

The result

The redesigned flow shipped ahead of Dyne's $1.6M seed round, and became part of the story the team told about the product's early traction.
