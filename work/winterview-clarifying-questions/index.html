Clarifying Questions

Role  Product DesignerCompany  WinterviewTimeline  2023–Present
+34%feature adoption

The problem

Winterview helps people practice for interviews using an AI interviewer. Early users could answer a question, but had no way to know whether they should expect a follow-up, or what a good one would even sound like. Practice sessions felt like a monologue instead of a conversation.

What I worked on

I designed the Clarifying Questions add-on, a feature that surfaces a smart follow-up question after a candidate's answer, the same way a real interviewer would probe for more detail.

  • Mapped the moments in a practice session where a real interviewer would typically ask a follow-up, and where the AI was staying silent instead
  • Designed the interaction so a suggested question appears without breaking the candidate's flow
  • Worked with engineering early on how the model's confidence in a question should affect what gets shown, so a low-confidence follow-up never appears polished enough to be mistaken for a sure thing
  • Ran a round of usability sessions with candidates preparing for real interviews, and iterated on tone and timing based on that feedback

The result

The feature shipped as an opt-in add-on to existing practice sessions.
